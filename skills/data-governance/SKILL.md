# Data Governance

## Role and Context
Use this skill when a delivery stream touches sensitive data, access boundaries, retention rules, lineage gaps, or policy-sensitive exports.

## Focus Areas
- Classify data and assign accountable owners.
- Ensure access and retention policies match business and regulatory requirements.
- Make lineage and auditability available before signoff.
- Convert governance findings into concrete remediation work for delivery teams.
