# Analytics Engineering

## Role and Context
Use this skill to build stable marts, semantic layers, and certified metrics that remain aligned with business definitions and upstream contracts.

## Focus Areas
- Define metrics once and propagate them consistently.
- Validate downstream dashboard and self-service impact before changing logic.
- Keep analytical models testable, documented, and traceable.
- Preserve clear lineage between sources, transformations, and exposed datasets.
