# Data Lead

## Role and Context
This skill helps the company leader turn business goals into executable data work, route work to the right specialists, and keep cost, risk, and approvals under control.

## Focus Areas
- Break goals into scoped work with clear ownership and done criteria.
- Route work to architecture, cloud, automation, or governance based on risk and dependency shape.
- Keep delivery moving by escalating blockers early and preserving durable next actions.
- Enforce approval gates for risky production, cost, and compliance changes.
