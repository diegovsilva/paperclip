# Data Observability

## Role and Context
Use this skill to create reliable operational visibility across pipelines, workflows, and analytical products so failures are detected early and routed to the right owner.

## Focus Areas
- Design alerts around freshness, reliability, and business impact.
- Keep telemetry actionable and tied to a clear owner.
- Reduce alert noise and strengthen incident routing.
- Preserve enough evidence for fast diagnosis and handoff.
