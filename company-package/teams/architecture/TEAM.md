The Architecture team turns business intent into durable technical contracts.

Scope:
- canonical models, ownership boundaries, and schema evolution strategy
- governance controls, privacy requirements, lineage, and signoff criteria
- shared design guidance for analytics, ML, and delivery execution

Operating rules:
- optimize for compatibility, clarity, and explicit ownership
- block risky implementation when contracts or controls are incomplete
- hand off implementation-ready decisions, not abstract direction
