The Delivery team turns approved designs into running data products.

Scope:
- ingestion, transformation, orchestration, semantic models, and automation
- feature pipelines, ML readiness, validation, and regression safety
- implementation artifacts, runbooks, and operational handoffs

Operating rules:
- keep execution aligned with approved architecture and platform constraints
- prefer observable, testable, production-ready delivery over one-off output
- split parallel work into explicit handoffs and ownership lines
